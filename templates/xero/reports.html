How are these numbers worked out?
  • Total owed is every unpaid invoice of the debtors (not counting written-off invoices or closed debtors), as it stands right now.
  • Being chased means the debtor is on the Debtors Action page. Waiting for a handover decision means it is on the Handover page. With the attorneys means a legal matter was approved — those debtors are taken off the clerks' pages and watched from the Lawyers page.
  • Money received is every payment made during the period, counted against the clerk the debtor belonged to when it was paid. Clerk's own collections is the part that counts as the clerk's work: they had contacted the debtor and it had not been handed over.
  • Recovery rate = money received ÷ (money received + written off + still owed at the end of the period).
  • Contacted means someone logged a call, WhatsApp, email or note on the debtor during the period.
  • A debtor's type is the one given to it by hand, otherwise worked out from a keyword in its name or project code (e.g. "abattoir"), otherwise "Not given a type yet".
